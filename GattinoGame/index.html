Gattino

A cozy co-op puzzle for two kittens.

Player 1 — Mouse Kitten chases your cursor. Only P1 can press buttons — park the cursor on one to hold it.
Player 2 — WASD / Arrows Move and push boxes onto buttons to hold them down permanently.

Collect all the fish, then both kittens sit on their own food bowl (marked 1 & 2). Boxes hold buttons down — players can't.
